# Direction update — Meta first, November 5, 2026

This update supersedes conflicting suggestions in earlier reference intake documents.

## Owner decisions

- Set the three previously supplied UI images aside as design direction. Some isolated details were decent; their overall layouts and styling are not targets. Retained files are archival only.
- The new video is someone else's feature mockup. Its broad concept is useful; Folio should develop its own stronger interaction and visual design rather than reproduce its form.
- Start the marketing manager with Meta. Future multi-platform connections may appear as inactive concepts or under Labs, clearly distinguished from working connections.
- Build a custom tactical marketing dashboard with deliberate, inspectable settings. The owner's concern is losing control of performance-affecting AI and automation options in Meta's own interface. Treat this as a product requirement, not a verified claim about Meta behavior or API access.
- Preserve the broader Folio agenda: small interaction improvements, performance, visuals, animations, mobile, sign-in, prospecting, knowledge base, themes, and later wellness exploration.
- Explore sound as part of interaction quality. Its exact role is undecided.
- Strong public showcases should emerge from ground-up product refinement and the best designed, most useful workflows.

## Proposed concept, not yet approved or implemented

Explore a campaign workspace with campaign navigation, a central performance/work area, and a contextual inspector with placement previews. Design connected/expired/disconnected account states and draft/review/publishing/result states. Keep consequential delivery settings visible and show a readable change summary before publishing. Verify actual Meta API capabilities later before implying a control is supported.

Prototype campaign creation, inspection, comparison, and editing as coherent workspace tasks rather than putting every task into a generic modal. Use plausible synthetic data and explicit concept labels for unbuilt integrations.

In parallel, maintain a Folio refinement track for navigation, typography, controls, loading/empty/error states, feedback, motion, mobile, and perceived speed. Propose sound as restrained, user-controlled cues with silent equivalents; do not make audio a dependency for understanding state.

This update records direction only. No canvas screens or production integration have been changed.
